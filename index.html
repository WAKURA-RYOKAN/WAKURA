About the Neighborhood — Ryogoku, Tokyo

Welcome to Ryogoku, one of Tokyo's most fascinating neighborhoods, where traditional Japanese culture and modern Tokyo come together.

Known as the heart of sumo culture, Ryogoku is home to the famous Ryogoku Kokugikan, where major sumo tournaments are held. The neighborhood is also rich in history, with attractions such as the Edo-Tokyo Museum and Kyu-Yasuda Garden nearby.

Take a stroll through the streets and you'll discover traditional restaurants, local shops, and the relaxed atmosphere of an authentic Tokyo neighborhood. Ryogoku is a wonderful place to experience a side of Tokyo that feels different from the busy tourist districts.

Getting Around

Ryogoku is conveniently served by both the JR Sobu Line and the Toei Oedo Line, making it an excellent base for exploring Tokyo.

From Ryogoku, you can easily access popular destinations such as Asakusa, Tokyo Skytree, Akihabara, Ueno, Ginza, and Shinjuku by train or subway.

Whether you are visiting Tokyo for sightseeing, food, Japanese culture, or simply looking for a convenient and relaxing place to stay, Ryogoku offers an ideal balance of traditional Tokyo charm and easy access to the city's major attractions.

✈️ From Narita Airport

  1. Take the Keisei Skyliner from Narita Airport to Ueno Station (the last stop).
  2. Walk to Ueno-Okachimachi Station and transfer to the Toei Oedo Line (about a 10-minute walk). There's an underground passageway connecting the stations, so you won't need to go above ground.
  3. Get off at Ryogoku Station (Oedo Line).
  4. It's just a couple of minutes' walk to WAKURA — follow the map below.

✈️ From Haneda Airport

  1. Take the Tokyo Monorail to its last stop.
  2. Transfer to the Toei Oedo Line subway (not the JR line).
  3. Get off at Ryogoku Station and follow the map below.
